# Triage Lens privacy policy

_Last updated: November 8, 2026_

Triage Lens is a Forge app that runs entirely on Atlassian's infrastructure ("Runs on Atlassian").

**What the app accesses.** When a user asks Triage Lens to triage a ticket, the app reads, on that user's behalf and with that user's permissions:
- the ticket's fields and similar resolved tickets in Jira
- matching Confluence pages
- the site's list of priority names

When the user confirms, the app updates the ticket's priority, assignee and labels, and adds a comment.

**What the app stores.** Nothing. Triage Lens has no database or storage. It does not copy, cache or keep any personal data or ticket content beyond the single request. It sends no data to any service outside Atlassian.

**Logs.** If an error occurs, the app writes a short technical error message to Atlassian's Forge logs. These are kept under Atlassian's standard retention.

**Responsibility.** The developer of Triage Lens, not Atlassian, is responsible for this app's handling of data.

**Contact.** For questions about this policy, use the support contact shown on the app's installation page.
